About

WHAT IS THE OUTSIDE?

The Outside is a one day festival in Accra. It happens every December. There is live music, food, a market, worship and talks, all in an open field instead of a church building.

It started in 2023 and it has grown every year since. The day runs from midday into the night, so you can come in the afternoon sun and stay for the music after dark.

Everyone is welcome

You do not need to belong to a church to come. Bring your friends and your family.

The festival is put together by The Oasis Collective, a group of young Christian creatives in Ghana.

Good to know

  • Come early. Gates open at 12:00 pm.
  • The last performance finishes at 9:00 pm.
  • Food and drinks are on sale at the market inside.
  • The festival is outdoors, so wear clothes and shoes you are comfortable in.
